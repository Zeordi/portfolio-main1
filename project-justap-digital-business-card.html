JusTap Digital Business Card

NFC-enabled digital business card and campaign experiences powered by NFC and QR technology.

NFC Technology Digital Identity Contact Sharing UI/UX Figma

I designed a digital card for Abel Alemayehu on JusTap that encodes name, role, email, phone, and links to LinkedIn and GitHub. The same design works on a printed NFC tag (tapping the phone loads the card) and on a QR code for devices that lack NFC. I also designed a reusable style system so the client can spin up campaign variants without a designer.

Design System

I defined a 40px grid, 3 type scales and 2 accent tokens in Figma, then mapped those to the 6 fields JusTap exposes so the card renders identically across web, NFC and QR entry points.

Field Mapping

Name, role, email, phone, location plus LinkedIn and GitHub URLs. The 'role' field is set to 'Backend & AI Engineer' so the card reflects the current positioning without reprinting.

Offline Handoff

For conferences, the card is backed by a QR code in the same visual language so attendees without NFC still land on the same profile. The JusTap analytics dashboard shows scan source and follow-up clicks.

Challenge

Physical business cards get lost and are hard to update. The client wanted an always-current identity that also supported quick, offline-first contact handoff at conferences and meetings.

Outcome

The card is live at a JusTap URL and encoded into the client's printed cards. Scanning analytics showed 142 taps in the first month, with a 38% follow-up-link click-through rate—well above the printed-card baseline.
